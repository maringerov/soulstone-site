Signs you've outgrown it

The setup that got you here is now slowing you down.

Most Shopify Plus stores don't fail outright — they accumulate complexity until growth quietly stalls. These are the patterns I see most often before a rearchitecture.

  • 01

    App sprawl & overlapping logic

    A dozen apps doing similar jobs, each adding scripts, cost, and points of failure.

  • 02

    Theme code no one fully understands

    Years of edits layered over a theme until small changes feel risky.

  • 03

    Slow storefront on mobile

    Bloat and third-party scripts dragging down the page where premium traffic converts.

  • 04

    Fragile checkout & purchase flow

    Customisations that break with every Shopify update or new campaign.

  • 05

    Data you can't trust

    Tracking that doesn't tie marketing spend back to real on-site revenue.

  • 06

    Releases that take too long

    Every change becomes a project because the foundation can't absorb it cleanly.
