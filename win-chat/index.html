The problem

Ask an AI assistant for the best product in your category. Does it name you?

Most premium brands we test are missing, while a competitor and a few review sites take the spot. AI answers lean on clear product data, trusted reviews and the lists that other sites publish. If your store does not give them that, they pick someone else.

What we do

Check, fix, get quoted, get talked about, measure.

  1. Check where you stand.

    We ask the questions your buyers ask, in their language, and show which brands get named and which sources get quoted.

  2. Fix your store.

    Product and brand data that AI can read, an llms.txt file, clean feeds for Google and Shopify's AI channels, and pages across markets that point to one source of truth.

  3. Give AI something to quote.

    Clear answer pages, comparison tables and real proof, written for people first.

  4. Get talked about.

    We find the reviews, lists and communities the answers rely on, and help you earn a place there.

  5. Measure every month.

    A fixed set of buyer questions, tracked over time, so you see progress in plain numbers.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this the same as SEO?

It builds on it. Search rankings still matter, but AI answers also weigh structured data, reviews and what other sites say about you.

How fast will we see results?

Store fixes are usually done within a month. Mentions in AI answers tend to move over two to three months.

Can you guarantee we will be recommended?

No one can. We show you exactly where you stand, do the work that moves it, and measure the result honestly.

Do we need to sell in the US for ChatGPT shopping?

For selling through ChatGPT via Shopify, yes. Being mentioned and linked in answers works in any market.
