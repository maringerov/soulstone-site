Get your brand recommended when shoppers ask AI.

The problem

Shoppers ask AI assistants first. Brands that are not cited are not considered.

A growing share of product research now happens inside a chat window. The assistant reads a handful of sources, picks a few brands and answers in one message. If your store is not in that answer, the shopper never sees it.

  • 01

    The answer is the shortlist

    An assistant names three or four brands and stops. There is no page two to be found on.

  • 02

    Assistants read differently

    They rely on clear product data, consistent facts and sources they can quote. Most Shopify stores were never set up for that.

  • 03

    You cannot see it happening

    Analytics show the visit, not the conversation that chose a competitor before it.

  • 04

    The gap compounds

    Brands that are cited today become the default recommendation tomorrow. Catching up gets harder each quarter.

How it works

Four steps from invisible to recommended.

The work starts with what the assistants already say about your category, then fixes the foundations on your Shopify store, then keeps watching as the assistants change.

  1. Audit

    We check how each assistant currently answers the questions your buyers ask, and where your brand appears or does not.

  2. Fix the foundations

    Structured product data, clear brand facts, crawl access and the pages assistants rely on, set up properly on your Shopify store.

  3. Earn the citations

    We strengthen the sources assistants quote: your own pages first, then the places that already talk about your category.

  4. Monitor and adjust

    Monthly checks on prompts, citations and recommendations, with changes as the assistants and your catalogue change.

Questions

What brands ask before they start.

Short answers on scope, timing and how Win chat fits next to the work you already do on search.

Which AI assistants do you cover?

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. We add others as they become relevant for shopping in your market.

Does this replace SEO?

No. It builds on it. Good search foundations help, but assistants weigh clear facts and quotable sources more heavily than rankings alone.

How long before we see results?

It depends on the category and on how much the assistants already know about the brand. The audit gives a realistic picture before any work starts.

Do we need to be on Shopify Plus?

No. The work applies to any Shopify store. Larger catalogues usually have more data to tidy up, which is why the audit comes first.
