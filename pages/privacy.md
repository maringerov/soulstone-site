soulstone.co uses Google Analytics 4 to see how the site is used. It only runs if you accept analytics cookies in the bar at the bottom of the page.

## What happens before you accept

Google Analytics is loaded in Google's Consent Mode with analytics storage denied. No analytics cookies are set until you choose Accept.

## What happens after you accept

Google Analytics sets its cookies (`_ga` and `_ga_` followed by the property id) and records which pages you visit. Your IP address is handled by Google as part of Google Analytics.

## No advertising

Ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation are always denied. Nothing on this site is used for advertising or ad personalisation.

## Where your choice is stored

Your choice is stored in your browser's localStorage under the key `ss_consent`, as either `granted` or `denied`. It stays until you change it or clear your browser's site data.

## Changing your choice

<a href="#cookie-choice" id="cookie-choice" data-ss-consent-reset>Change cookie choice</a>. This clears the stored choice and shows the bar again. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings.

## The teardown request form

What you enter in the teardown form on the homepage and on [/teardown/](/teardown/) (name, email, store URL, revenue band, country, the problem you describe and the page you sent it from) goes to a Google Apps Script owned by Soulstone and is stored in a Google Sheet there. It is used to reply to your request and for nothing else: no list, no newsletter, no sharing. Ask at the address below if you want it deleted.
