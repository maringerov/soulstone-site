The story

How an overgrown Plus store became a growth engine.

01

The challenge

Strong demand, a store that couldn't keep up.

A premium watch and accessories brand was investing heavily in brand, paid media, and partnerships — and it showed in the traffic. But the Shopify Plus store had grown over several years into a tangle of apps and theme edits.

Pages were slow on mobile, the purchase flow leaked customers at checkout, and the team couldn't clearly tie marketing spend back to on-site revenue. Growth had quietly plateaued despite rising attention.

02

The approach

Rebuild the foundation without going dark.

We started with a short diagnostic to find where revenue was actually leaking, then sequenced a focused rebuild — highest-leverage fixes first, no big-bang replatform.

App sprawl was consolidated, the storefront and theme were rebuilt for speed, and the checkout and tracking were cleaned up so every change shipped incrementally while the store kept selling.

03

The outcome

A faster store the team can scale on.

Within one focused engagement, the store loaded faster on mobile, more traffic converted, and the brand finally had one source of truth connecting marketing performance to on-site behaviour.

More importantly, the foundation is now legible and maintainable — the team ships changes without breaking things, and growth is no longer capped by the architecture.

“The store finally keeps up with the brand. It's faster, we understand what's driving revenue, and we can ship changes without holding our breath.”
E-commerce Lead · Premium watch & accessories brand
